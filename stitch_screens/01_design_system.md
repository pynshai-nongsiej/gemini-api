## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a disciplined, Swiss-modern editorial philosophy built specifically for candidates preparing for elite state administrative examinations (MPSC LDA and DSC West Khasi Hills). The design aesthetic is scholarly, razor-focused, and unencumbered by visual noise. It deliberately rejects childish gamification, skeuomorphic study tropes, paper textures, and loud dopamine loops in favor of calm, high-density intellectual agency.

Key visual attributes include:
- **Quiet Editorial Authority:** Generous typographical whitespace, hairline dividing rules, tabular clarity, and surgical typography that mimics elite reference journals and Swiss modern catalogues.
- **Dignified Progression:** Gamification (streaks, performance analytics, XP, and syllabus mastery metrics) is executed with the quiet precision of financial data or institutional indices—using restrained typography, micro-badges, and hairline trackers rather than flashy arcade badges.
- **Stark Informational Contrast:** A warm, neutral editorial ground paired with deep neutral ink ensures non-fatiguing legibility across sustained 10-hour study sessions.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an editorial light environment with strict functional assignments. Color is never applied decoratively; it serves strictly as a taxonomy marker, systemic indicator, or precise feedback signal.

### Surface & Ink Tokens
- **Background (Editorial Ground):** `#FAFAF8` — A quiet, warm editorial white that softens eye strain compared to clinical `#FFFFFF`.
- **Surface (Card & Panel Base):** `#FFFFFF` — Used sparingly to elevate active modules, response areas, and test panes over the base ground.
- **Text Primary (Deep Ink):** `#18181B` — High-contrast zinc neutral for crisp headings, core body copy, and primary questions.
- **Text Secondary (Sub-editorial):** `#71717A` — Supporting meta-information, chapter labels, and contextual subtitles.
- **Text Muted (De-emphasized):** `#A1A1AA` — Placeholder copy, inactive states, and timestamp markers.
- **Borders & Rules:** `#E4E4E7` — Precise 1px structural dividing lines.

### Functional & Gamification Accents
- **Primary Accent:** `#2563EB` (Royal Blue) — System focal points, primary action triggers, and active study pathways.
- **Primary Accent Light:** `#EFF6FF` — Soft wash for selected states and active row backgrounds.
- **XP & Analytical Accent:** `#7C3AED` (Deep Violet) — Precision metrics, XP allocation, and mastery score telemetry.
- **Success:** `#16A34A` — Verified answers, target syllabus completion, and upward trends.
- **Warning:** `#D97706` — Revision backlogs, timed warnings, and threshold alerts.
- **Error:** `#DC2626` — Incorrect answer choices, failed criteria, and critical study lapses.

### Curriculum Subject Taxonomy
Each subject domain receives a discrete, unmuted classification hue used only in micro-badges, syllabus indices, and category tags:
- **General English:** `#2563EB`
- **General Knowledge & Meghalaya Affairs:** `#16A34A`
- **Aptitude & Elementary Mathematics:** `#D97706`
- **General Intelligence & Reasoning:** `#7C3AED`
- **Computer Theory & Practical Literacy:** `#0891B2`

## Typography

Typography prioritizes legibility, tight vertical alignment, and structured data handling. 

- **Display & Headings (Manrope):** Clean geometric letterforms deliver structural poise to syllabus modules, section dividers, and examination dashboards. Tighter letter-spacing is enforced on display sizes to evoke contemporary Swiss publishing.
- **Reading & User Interface (Geist):** Handles high-density multiple-choice passages, detailed solutions, and telemetry numbers with neutral precision. Geist’s balanced proportions allow prolonged concentration without optical fatigue.
- **Tabular & Metric Notation:** Numerical readouts, countdown timers, accuracy percentages, and XP markers utilize tabular lining properties within Geist to preserve vertical columnar alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon architectural rigor, relying on a slim, permanent desktop navigation rail and an uncluttered content plane governed by clear horizontal rhythm.

### Grid & Architecture
- **Desktop Navigation Rail:** Fixed width of `220px` (`13.75rem`), border-right anchored by a crisp `1px solid #E4E4E7` divider.
- **Primary Content Canvas:** Centered, reading-optimized fluid grid capped at `1200px` (`75rem`).
- **Reading & Evaluation Lane:** Multi-choice questions, comprehension sections, and explanation dialogues are restrained to a strict max-width of `704px` (`44rem`) to maintain comfortable typographical line lengths (55–75 characters per line).

### Form Factors & Adaptability
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fixed narrow left rail (`220px`), expansive open canvas, dual-pane examination layouts (question text left, response telemetry right).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Navigation collapses to a top-level editorial header (`48px` height); padding shifts to `1.5rem`; side-by-side components stack sequentially.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Clean vertical single-column stream. Margins drop to `1rem`. Fixed bottom utilities house primary submission triggers, streak indicators, and timer status bars.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects layered skeuomorphic drop shadows and blur effects. Depth is achieved strictly through **flat tonal planes and hairline outlines**.

- **Level 0 (Base Ground):** `#FAFAF8` — The primary workspace.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface):** `#FFFFFF` framed with `1px solid #E4E4E7`. No ambient shadow is used. Content boundaries rely on crisp structural alignment.
- **Level 2 (Active Modals & Context Flyouts):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-subtle, uncolored boundary shadow (`0 4px 12px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06)`) and a `1px solid #E4E4E7` container border.
- **Focus & Selection Emphasis:** Achieved exclusively through high-contrast boundary transitions (shifting a border from `#E4E4E7` to `#2563EB`) or clean tonal tint fills (`#EFF6FF`).

## Shapes

The interface maintains crisp geometry to uphold its editorial, document-first demeanor. Structural containers and form inputs utilize subtle, restrained corner radii (`4px` / `0.25rem`). 

Full pill styling (`rounded-full`) is reserved strictly for micro-badges, discrete streak pills, and subject tags to create clear visual contrast between functional cards and informative labels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#2563EB` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` corner radius, font weight 500. Zero drop shadow. Active state shifts to `#1D4ED8`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4E4E7`, text `#18181B`. Hover transitions border to `#71717A`.
- **Ghost / Action:** Transparent background, text `#71717A`, subtle `#EFF6FF` background on hover with text `#2563EB`.

### Chips & Subject Badges
- **Subject Badges:** Subtle background tint (e.g., `#2563EB` at 8% opacity) paired with a solid font color matching the subject token. Shape is `rounded-full`, padding `2px 8px`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Status & Streak Pills:** `#FAFAF8` background, hairline border `#E4E4E7`, containing an icon (streak flame or clock) and a tabular figure rendered in `#18181B`.

### Lists & Examination Options
- **Default Option Item:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E4E4E7` border, `4px` radius, padding `14px 18px`. Left side displays an uppercase key (A, B, C, D) in a muted neutral square.
- **Selected State:** Border color `#2563EB`, background `#EFF6FF`, key badge shifts to solid `#2563EB` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Answer Verified (Review Mode):** Correct option takes border `#16A34A` and fill `#F0FDF4`. Incorrect user selection takes border `#DC2626` and fill `#FEF2F2`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp, understated `16px` squared checks and round radios. Border `1.5px solid #A1A1AA`. On active check, background fills with `#2563EB` showing a crisp white tick or pip.

### Input Fields
- Flat `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E4E4E7` border, `4px` radius, height `40px`, horizontal padding `12px`. Focus state: border shifts to `#2563EB` with a `0 0 0 1px #2563EB` outline ring. No glow effects.

### Cards & Division
- Avoid bulky container boxes. Separate discrete modules using single `1px solid #E4E4E7` rules and generous `gutter-xl` whitespace. When a surface card is strictly necessary, it remains flat `#FFFFFF` with a `1px solid #E4E4E7` frame.

### Performance & Telemetry Strip (Gamification)
- Sits at the top of the test module or in the navigation rail. Displays quantitative variables: Daily Streak, Syllabus Covered %, Target MPSC Mock Score, and XP Accrued. Styled cleanly like a financial ticker with muted Geist labels and prominent tabular figures.
